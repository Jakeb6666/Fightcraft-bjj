Fightcraft BJJ is a Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu academy in Mansfield Woodhouse, led by black belt Professor Shane Mills. Training is about more than learning techniques: it’s about building confidence, staying curious and helping each other improve.

From your first time stepping onto the mats to developing your game, the focus is on learning, consistency and respect.

First time on the mats?

Everyone
starts somewhere.

You don’t need to be fit, flexible or experienced to ask about your first class. The hardest part is often just walking through the door.

Ask your first-timer questions ↗
01

Get in touch

Tell the team you’re new and ask which class is best to try first.

02

Check what to bring

Ask whether you need a gi, or if you can train in comfortable sportswear for your first session.

03

Focus on listening, learning and enjoying the experience. Nobody expects you to know everything.

04

Small improvements add up. Take your time, train consistently and celebrate your progress.

Wondering if BJJ is for you? Here are a few things worth knowing before you get in touch.

Absolutely worth asking! Let the academy know you’re new so they can guide you toward the most suitable class and explain what to expect.

You don’t need to get in shape before starting. Begin at your own pace and let your fitness develop alongside your skills.

Ask Fightcraft what to wear for your first session. Requirements can differ between gi and no-gi classes, so check before buying kit.

Timetables and fees can change. Contact the academy directly for the latest schedule, prices, trial options and membership details.

Competition isn’t the only reason to train BJJ. Ask the coaches about recreational training and the options available to you.
